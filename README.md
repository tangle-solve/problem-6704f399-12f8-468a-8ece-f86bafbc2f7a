### CHEM-000340: Origin of unequal nitrate-aerosol yields from the two phellandrenes
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Identify particle-phase pathways explaining α-/β-phellandrene nitrate-oxidation yield differences, closing carbon and nitrate balances with resolved products.
- **Status:** A 2025 chamber study found maximum aerosol yields near 35% and 60%, respectively, despite similarities in measured gas chemistry. The condensed-phase explanation remains incomplete.
